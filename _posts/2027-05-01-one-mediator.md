> "For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus, who gave himself as a ransom for all."
> ~ **1 Timothy 2:5–6**

The sentence is not a metaphor about communication skills. Paul is naming the only one who stands between God and men, and he names him as the man who gave himself as a ransom. The Second London Confession, in the chapter on Christ the mediator, stays there. The Son is prophet, priest, and king. He reveals the Father. He reconciles. He governs. He does this as the one mediator of the covenant of grace. There is not a second, for the soul, in a collar or in a headset.

I have reached for the comparison this craft loves, and it is false. The report is not an atonement. The debrief is not a cross. The tester is not a priest of the system. A witness in a small and creaturely sense can tell the truth about a boundary. He cannot stand in the gap between God and the house, and he should be afraid of the voice he uses when he tries.

![A sealed letter on the near stone, a gold cross across a dark gap.](/assets/img/site/mediator-gap.jpg)

### What he actually is

He is a messenger with a limited sight and a written charge. He entered because someone with authority asked. He returns what the trial showed, including what it did not show. Then he leaves. The [National Cyber Security Centre's public guidance on penetration testing](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/penetration-testing) is useful at exactly this altitude. It speaks to the organization that buys a test, and it treats the outcome as something the buyer must be able to receive and use. That is the right size. The product is an account, given back to the keeper. It is not a sacramental act, and it is not a substitute for the keeper remaining the keeper.

[NIST's guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final) belongs in the same modest place. It describes an assessment as a circumstance of an institution, with a report as one of its parts. I am not inviting anyone to turn that guide into a liturgy, and I am not walking its methods on this page. I am refusing the promotion. A standard that tells you to write down what you did and what you found is a discipline of speech. Discipline of speech is a creaturely good. It does not make the speaker a mediator.

The gospel is the power of God for salvation. An honest report can serve a neighbor. It can keep a thief from being the first to know. It remains a creaturely good. It does not justify the operator, the keeper, or the institution. Peace with God is through our Lord Jesus Christ. A clean quarter is not that peace. A missed finding is not damnation. The man who must be justified by his report will lie in his report, because the report has been asked to do what only the ransom can do. The man who is justified by Christ can write the limit of what he saw. He does not need the house to be saved by the document in order to be saved himself.

### The voice that steals the office

The theft is usually tonal. The panes multiply. The room goes quiet. The speaker stops saying "we returned this" and starts saying "this is who you are." He assigns ranks of guilt. He offers a path back into his good graces, which is a retainment. He implies that until he speaks again, the house is in a kind of exile. None of that is mediation in the doctrinal sense. It is a costume. The costume works because people know, from the law on the heart, that judgment is real and that they want someone to stand between them and it. The hunger is not stupid. It is aimed at Christ. A tester who stands in front of the hunger and offers himself is not being pastoral. He is being a rival.

There is a kinder theft, and it is still a theft. The operator truly cares. He stays after the engagement in the role of a chaplain of risk, interpreting every event, refusing to let the keeper decide, softening and hardening the truth according to what he thinks the soul of the institution can bear. Love does not do this. Love tells the truth and leaves the person able to act. The shepherd of a church is the pastor, not the consultant. The head of a house is the keeper, not the guest who tried the locks. One mediator, for the soul. For the boundary, a witness, and then the witness goes home.

I will not allegorize the incarnation into an engagement. The Son took on flesh. A tester takes on a role for a contracted time. The resemblance is verbal and it is a trap. "He became like the adversary" is already too much incense for a statement of work. He was authorized to resemble a method, for a time, so a friend would know before a thief. That is all. The one who became man, who gave himself as a ransom, is not the patron saint of the resemblance. He is the Lord in whose name the resemblance has to end.

### The sentence the report may use

Returned. Not returned. Inferred. Unknown. This is the limit of the sight. This is the name of the keeper who must act. This is the hour the access dies. Those sentences can be severe. Severity is not priesthood. A severe truth, handed across the table to the person who can use it, is the whole of the office. Anything added so that the room will look at the messenger has left the office and started a small religion. The religion will have recurring revenue. It will not have a cross that saves.

Christ remains the one mediator. The rest of us deliver reports.
