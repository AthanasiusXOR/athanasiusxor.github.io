> "His understanding is beyond measure."
> ~ **Psalm 147:5**
>
> "And no creature is hidden from his sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must give account."
> ~ **Hebrews 4:13**

The Second London Confession says that God's knowledge is infinite, infallible, and independent of the creature. He knows because he is God. He does not know by installing a collector, and he does not know more truly when the collector is expensive. The creature who wants that knowledge for himself does not become more like God by wanting it. He becomes a parody. The parody has a product category now. It is called visibility, and it is sold as if the remaining darkness were a moral failure.

A keeper needs light. That sentence is not piety against the work. A steward who cannot see the room cannot keep it. The lust this essay judges is the further sentence, the one the brochure adds: that enough light, of the right kind, from enough places, becomes sight in the divine sense. It does not. Omniscience is his. The dashboard is a lamp. Lamps are good. Calling a lamp an eye that misses nothing is a doctrine, and the doctrine is false.

![A lamp and an open book with a gold cross, and a field of dim lights behind them.](/assets/img/site/being-lamp.jpg)

### What the confession is refusing

"Great is our Lord, and abundant in power; his understanding is beyond measure" (Psalm 147:5). The measure is not a storage quota. Hebrews says no creature is hidden from his sight, and the point of that sentence is judgment, not telemetry. We give account to him. He is not giving account to a retention policy. Job asks where wisdom is found, and the deep says it is not in me. God understands the way to it, and he knows its place (Job 28). The knowledge that searches the heart is not a feature a house can buy, and it is not a feature a tester can lend.

This matters before anyone picks a sensor, because the craft has learned to talk about gaps as if they were theological. A gap in the log is treated as a kind of fall. A complete log is treated as a kind of righteousness. Neither is true. A gap is a limit of a creaturely instrument. A full disk is still a creaturely instrument, and it has not seen the heart. The operator who enjoys the gaps as a private kingdom, the places he can stand because the keeper cannot, has already changed stewardship into a hiding. The vendor who offers the client a counterfeit of divine sight has changed a lamp into a claim about God. Both are answered by the same doctrine. His knowledge does not depend on the creature. Theirs does.

### Light the house actually needs

The tools are real, and they should be named as what they are.

[Zeek](https://zeek.org/) is a witness on a network. It can say, within the vantage it was given, what passed. [Sysmon](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/sysmon) is a witness on a host. It can say, within the events it was told to keep, what a machine recorded. [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) is a shared vocabulary for adversary behavior, so that a keeper and a tester can name a thing without inventing a private dialect for it. None of these sees as God sees. A witness has a place to stand. A vocabulary has a history and a bias toward what its authors have already met. Both are lamps. Both can be faithful. Neither is Hebrews 4:13.

David Bianco's [Pyramid of Pain](https://detect-respond.blogspot.com/2013/03/the-pyramid-of-pain.html) is useful here because it refuses the fantasy of one kind of sight. Some indicators are cheap to collect and cheap for an adversary to change. Some are dearer, because they describe how a person works and not merely a string that person used. The pyramid is not a spirituality. It is a piece of prudence about cost. I cite it for the modesty. A house that has bought the cheapest sight and calls it omniscience has not read its own instrument. A house that climbs the pyramid and then calls the summit the knowledge of God has only moved the false doctrine up a step.

The laboratory where I am willing to let an instrument be looked at, apart from anyone's house, is [Geneva](https://github.com/AthanasiusXOR/geneva-ludus). It is a range. It can show what a witness returns and what it does not. It cannot show the heart. That limit is the point of keeping a range at all. A man practices sight where the names are not a neighbor's, and he learns the size of the lamp before he carries the lamp into a church, a clinic, or a firm.

### The two sins that wear this doctrine

The first is the tester's. He has been in the places the log did not love. He knows a path the keeper's ordinary view did not render. That knowledge is a creaturely good when it is returned. It becomes a private kingdom when he keeps it so that he can remain the man who sees. The pleasure is old. It is the pleasure of being necessary because other people are in the dark. Omniscience, parodied, always comes with a court. The tester appoints himself the court. The confession will not have it. The sight was never his to hoard, and the sight he does have is not the sight he is imitating.

The second is the keeper's, and the seller's. A board is shown a wall of panes and told that the house is now known. The unknown is omitted because it does not glow. What was inferred is dressed as what was seen. The buyer wanted peace, and he was sold a feeling that resembles the verse about nothing hidden. The feeling is not the verse. Hebrews is a word about the God to whom we give account. A quarterly review is a word about sensors. To confuse them is to train a congregation, or a company, to fear the wrong eye and to trust the wrong eye. They will hide from the dashboard what they should have confessed to God, and they will confess to the dashboard what God has not asked a machine to absolve.

Build what the house truly needs. A clinic needs a different lamp than a trading floor. A congregation of eighty does not need the estate that flatters an operator. Need is a moral word before it is a procurement word. The light of nature and Christian prudence can size a lamp. They cannot turn the lamp into the understanding that is beyond measure. Sufficiency belongs to Scripture, not to retention. A man who keeps adding collectors until the feeling of omniscience arrives will not arrive there. He will arrive at a louder room and a quieter conscience.

### What to ask instead

Ask what the witness can actually say, and from where. Ask what it was not given. Ask who is allowed to stand in the gap, and for how long, and to whom the standing is confessed. Ask whether the report says "returned," "not returned," and "inferred," or whether it says "we know." The first is stewardship. The second is a borrowed accent from a knowledge that does not borrow.

And ask it of yourself if you are the one who likes the dark. The gap is not your realm. It is a limit in a made thing, under a God who is not limited, and under a keeper who is owed the truth about both. You may carry a lamp. You may say where it fails. You may not call yourself the eye.

The lab can uncover a method. A sensor can uncover a trace. Neither searches the heart. That searching is already under way, and it is not yours to complete or to sell.
