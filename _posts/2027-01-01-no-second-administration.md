December asked who answered when a program called a library by name. January asks the next question. Once something is running, who is giving it work?

An estate has an administration. Tasks are issued. Machines check in. Work is scheduled. The owner can open a console and see that government. A second channel that issues tasks the owner did not schedule is another administration, wearing the quiet of the house.

The plate at the top is that arrangement. One console faces the owner. A second channel runs beside the racks, into the same machines, and does not land on the glass.

> "You will recognize them by their fruits. Are grapes gathered from thornbushes, or figs from thistles? So, every healthy tree bears good fruit, but the diseased tree bears bad fruit."
> ~ **Matthew 7:16–17**

## One mediator, one console

This field-post has kept the covenants in their own names. The new covenant is a real act of God, and it is not a second people still running beside Christ after the Son has come. There is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus. The administration is visible. They shall all know the Lord, from the least of them to the greatest. A hidden government beside that knowledge would be another covenant, and another covenant is a smaller Christ.

![One cup on the seat, and a broken path that does not arrive.](/assets/img/site/one-mediator.jpg)

The technical error has the same shape. The console is a true administration. It names the tools the owner chose, the jobs those tools run, and the people allowed to start them. A channel that tasks the same hosts, on a rhythm the console did not set, is another party. An inventory that blesses the approved tools and never asks who else is giving orders has flattened two administrations into one. The tools were real. The party giving the orders was a different party.

Call the second channel command and control and you have named the trade. The name does less work than people think. Management is already a remote task. The failure is a remote task the steward cannot see, issuing work the steward did not authorize. Nehemiah's guard was posted where the wall was. A guard who watches the front gate and never asks about the path behind the house is watching one administration while two are in the house.

## What the owner should be able to see

The public claim stops at recognition.

Fruit is the test the Lord gave, and it is the test that survives a trusted name. A healthy tool bears the work the owner queued. A second channel bears other fruit: tasks the console did not create, a rhythm of contact the estate's own tools do not keep, a host that answers to a schedule nobody in the house wrote down. You do not have to join the second channel to ask the question. You have to be able to see the work.

The record of tasks and the record of contact are where the question is asked. A list of approved products is the wrong document for that question. A signed manager can be the only government in the house. A signed manager can also be the door a stranger is already using. The name on the tool is a party. The fruit tells you whether a second party has joined it.

How a channel is hidden, how its contact is dressed up as ordinary traffic, and how a server is stood up to give orders do not belong on this field-post. They belong, when they belong at all, inside an authorized engagement with a scope and a way to undo the damage. The judgment belongs here. A control that trusts the product and ignores the fruit will pass a house that already has two governments.

## The repair is sight

The repair is an account of the work. What ran. Who queued it. Whether the rhythm matches the administration on the books. Where the account breaks, the second channel is already the finding. The report names it so it can be retired. A quieter house is the product.

The decree of God is the purpose, and it is not a figure for a beacon. The covenants are how that purpose is administered in history, each under its own name, until Christ, who is the one mediator. An estate's console is a much smaller thing. It is still an administration. It should be the only one.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
