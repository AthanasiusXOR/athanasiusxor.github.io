The test can prove it was serious by the size of the mess. An outage. A spectacle. A ministry that cannot do its work on the morning after the engagement, offered as evidence that the team went deep. The plate at the top sets the blade at the edge of the wheat. The field is still standing. The blade is not a trophy for having cut it down.

> "But he said, 'No, lest in gathering the weeds you root up the wheat along with them. Let both grow together until the harvest, and at harvest time I will tell the reapers, Gather the weeds first and bind them in bundles to be burned, but gather the wheat into my barn.'"
> ~ **Matthew 13:29–30**

## The parable stays a parable

The Lord is speaking about the kingdom, the sons of the evil one, and a harvest that is his. He is not issuing a policy that a known weakness should be left in a house because remediation is inconvenient. A security program that baptizes an open door as wheat and tares has stolen a parable to avoid a repair. The distance has to be said before the application, or the application becomes an excuse.

What the parable does forbid, and what an engagement must hear, is a zeal that pulls up the field. The servants wanted to gather the weeds immediately. The master refused a gathering that would root up the wheat with them. The harvest is his. The servants are not told that weeds are wheat. They are told that their haste can destroy what they were sent to protect.

## The blade

Operational care was already one of the five guardrails. Going deep is not a reason to knock the environment over. The offensive pressure exists so the keepers can see a weakness under conditions they granted. A weakness shown by wrecking the ministry has made the proof more expensive than the finding. The congregation that cannot meet, the clerks who cannot be paid, the counsel that cannot be reached, are the wheat. The weakness is the weed. A demonstration that treats them as the same plant has failed the stewardship even if the report is admired.

The other failure keeps the blade in the shed and calls the shed patience. A weed that can be pulled without destroying the wheat is pulled. The door that can be shut without ending the ministry is shut. The access that can be ended without scattering the flock is ended. "Let both grow" is the Lord's word about a harvest the servants do not schedule. It is not the keeper's word about a hole he has been shown and can close. December's hearer, who files the warning and does not move, does not get to cite this parable on the way out of the meeting.

## What may be broken

Some pressure has a cost. April already said harm that cannot be avoided, if the truth is to be known at all, is kept small, told at once, and undone. The public claim stops there. This page will not describe how to press a house or how to break it safely. The judgment is the blade in the plate. It is at the edge. The wheat is identified and left standing. The weed is not renamed. A test that has to ruin the field to be believed has loved the proving more than the field.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
