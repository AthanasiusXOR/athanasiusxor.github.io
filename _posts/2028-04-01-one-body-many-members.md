The assessment can be scored like a match. The testers won. The defenders lost. The readout becomes the story of a member that needed no one else. The plate at the top is the arrangement the match forgets. The lamps are set in one body. A member who will not stand there has already said the sentence Paul forbids.

> "The eye cannot say to the hand, 'I have no need of you,' nor again the head to the feet, 'I have no need of you.'"
> ~ **1 Corinthians 12:21**

## The eye and the hand

God arranged the members. A security program does not get to improve on that by crowning one craft. The offensive work is a member. Its gift is pressure under a grant, so the body can see a weakness while there is still time to take warning. The defenders are a member. Their gift is the watch on the morning after, the recognition, the standing guard. The keepers are a member. Their gift is the charge, the authority to open the door and the duty to hear. The eye that says it has no need of the hand is the red team that keeps the finding as a win against blue. The hand that says it has no need of the eye is the defender who will not receive an emulation because the emulation came from outside the watch.

Neither sentence serves the body. A finding scored against the people who must use it has left the body it was given to. A watch that will not hear the eye has chosen blindness and called it loyalty. May said the trumpet is for the guard. The range essay said concealment from the keeper is the range scaled past the work. This essay names the pride underneath both. The member wants to be the body.

## Arranged, not hired as rivals

The plate is the correction. The members are already set together. The work is not to invent a unity after the match. The work is to stop speaking as if the match were the point. Deconfliction, the joint readout, the finding written so a defender can act, are the ordinary manners of one body. They are not a loss of nerve. An emulation that blue was never meant to understand is a private religion. A blue team that measures its worth by how little the emulation found, and hides the conditions so the finding cannot be true, is the same religion in the other chair.

The church already knows this shape. One member with a spectacular gift can empty a congregation of every other gift and call the emptiness anointing. Elders exist in the plural so the body is not one mouth. The lone operator, tester or defender or admin, who needs no witness and no keeper, is that mouth. Two or three witnesses were required for a charge. A body is required for a finding to become a watch. The spectacular member still has to be able to be questioned.

## What the body owes

The public claim stops at recognition. The offensive member owes a finding the other members can receive. The defending member owes a hearing that is not a rivalry. The keeper owes an arrangement in which those two cannot avoid each other until the warning is either taken or refused in the open. How either craft does its technical work does not belong on this page. The judgment does. The lamps in the plate are one body because they were set that way. A member that steps out to be admired has all the light it wants, and the body is darker for the admiration.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
