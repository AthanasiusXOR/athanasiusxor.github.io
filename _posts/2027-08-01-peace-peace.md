The test is over and the room wants a sentence it can repeat. The path was real. The sentence on offer is that the wound is slight. The plate at the top puts the word across the crack. The crack continues under the word and out across the floor.

> "They have healed the wound of my people lightly, saying, 'Peace, peace,' when there is no peace."
> ~ **Jeremiah 6:14**

## Who says it

Jeremiah's prophets were not ignorant of the wound. They spoke over it. The readout has the same voices, and they do not all wear the same badge. The dashboard was green before the engagement and someone would like it green after. The vendor's promise was peace, and the demonstration is an inconvenience to the promise. The slide existed before the test and would prefer not to be rewritten. The keeper is tired. A word like theoretical, or accepted, or low, will end the meeting.

None of those speakers has to lie in a dramatic way. Healing lightly is a tone. The finding remains in the document. The sentence that leads the document says the people may rest. The offensive work was commissioned so a falsehood the architecture tells about itself could be retired. A debrief that restores the falsehood, politely, has spent the engagement to purchase the prophets' sentence.

## What the pressure was

An emulation inside a grant is not a rumor about what an adversary might someday attempt. It is a pressure the keeper asked for, bounded, and able to be ended. When that pressure walks a path, the path is no longer a theory. Calling it theoretical after it has been walked is how a real wound is given a small name. The defenders who were told to treat it as slight will watch the wrong place. May already said the trumpet has to be audible. This essay is about the trumpet that is sounded and then explained until nobody gets up.

There is a cruelty that goes the other direction, and April already refused it. A report can humiliate a person or wreck a room to prove the testers were serious. Peace, peace is not cured by panic, panic. The true sentence is the size of the wound. What failed. Under what conditions. What the repair has to accomplish. The keepers can bear a true sentence. They cannot be helped by a small one.

## A slight healing

The repair that changes the color of the finding and leaves the house is the light healing. The control is renamed. The priority is lowered. The risk is accepted by the person who does not live with it. The crack is still in the wall. The banner is new.

The public claim stops at recognition. How a path is opened does not belong here. The judgment does. If the word on the banner cannot be said in front of the crack without shame, the banner is the false prophecy. Take it down. Say the size of the wound. Let the people who keep the house decide what peace would actually require.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
