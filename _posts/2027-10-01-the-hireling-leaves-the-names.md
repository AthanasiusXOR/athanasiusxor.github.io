The invoice is paid. The report is delivered. The people who pressed the house are gone, and the names they touched are still in the house. The plate at the top is that departure. The book is on the floor. The gate is open. Nobody is on the path.

> "He who is a hired hand and not a shepherd, who does not own the sheep, sees the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and flees, and the wolf snatches them and scatters them. He flees because he is a hired hand and cares nothing for the sheep."
> ~ **John 10:12–13**

## Paid, and gone

An engagement is allowed to end. A grant has an edge and a date, and the stranger who was invited does not get to move in. That limit is real, and a later essay will say it. The hireling is a different failure. He was paid to care, and the care ended at the document. The offensive work produced a true finding. The finding named a weakness that touches neighbors: mail, money, counsel, the names of people who cannot afford to be known by strangers. Then the author of the finding left the book on the floor and called the leaving professional.

The shepherd in John's sentence owns a relation the hired hand does not. A consultant does not own a congregation, and should not pretend to. The sentence still bites. Caring nothing for the sheep is visible. It looks like a readout with no way to be questioned, a trophy that cannot be translated into a repair, a team that will not answer when the keeper calls three weeks later and asks what the third finding meant. The PDF is not care. Care stays long enough for the keeper to understand, and long enough for the defenders to take up the watch the finding described.

## When the wolf comes

The wolf in the engagement is not the assessor. The assessor was invited. The wolf is the later intrusion that was not invited, walking a weakness the report already named, or a weakness the report obscured with a slight healing. The hired hand sees that shape and is already gone. He saw something like it during the test and treated the seeing as the product. When the real thing comes, the flock learns that the person who understood the path is not a person they can reach.

The keepers are not excused by the hireling's flight. The charge was theirs before the engagement and remains theirs after it. A church that hired a firm so the elders would not have to look has hired a substitute shepherd. March already said the elders must be able to see who holds the names. This essay adds the person who looked once, was paid, and left the elders with paper they cannot use. Both the flight and the abdication are the same indifference. The sheep are still there.

## Stay until it is understood

The repair is presence of a limited kind. The engagement ends. The accountability for what was found does not evaporate with the invoice. The testers owe a report that can be acted on, and a way to answer a true question about it. The keepers owe the ongoing watch, the questions, and the refusal to treat the firm as a shepherd. Neither party is allowed to be the hired hand who cares nothing.

How a weakness is turned into an entry does not belong on this field-post. The judgment does. If the book of names is on the floor and the people who wrote it cannot be asked what they saw, the engagement produced a hireling. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. No assessor is that shepherd. An assessor can at least refuse to flee from the explanation.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
