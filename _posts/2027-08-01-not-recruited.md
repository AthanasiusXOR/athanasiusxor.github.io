> "As for you, you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good."
> ~ **Genesis 50:20**

Joseph is not offering the brothers a partnership. They meant evil. God meant good. The same act is truly theirs and truly under his hand, and the two meanings are not the same meaning. The Second London Confession, on providence, says that God upholds, directs, disposes, and governs all creatures, actions, and things, from the greatest to the least, by his most wise and holy providence, to the praise of his glory. He governs second causes. He is not the author of sin. He is not waiting to be asked onto the engagement.

That last clause is the one this craft keeps trying to edit. A test is described as if heaven had joined the team. A finding is delivered as if it were the punishment the house had coming. A red team is spoken of as providence with a badge. Joseph will not lend the sentence. The brothers are not vindicated because God brought good. The tester is not deputized because God governs the thief. He is not recruited. Neither are you.

### Resemblance is not recruitment

An authorized test resembles an adversary so the keeper can learn from a friend what he might otherwise learn from a thief. The resemblance is a circumstance of the charge. It is not a claim that God has endorsed the method, and it is not a claim that the method and the crime are the same act. Providence governs both the crime and the test. It does not collapse them. The thief means the trophy. The tester, if he is still a tester, means the truth returned to the keeper. God can govern a wicked will without becoming wicked, and he can govern a lawful test without becoming a member of the project. The moment the project talks as if he had signed the statement of work, the project has made a mascot of the doctrine.

The public tools of resemblance should be kept at that size. [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) is a vocabulary for adversary behavior. It lets a test say what it is trying to resemble, in words a defender can share, instead of in a private legend. [Caldera](https://caldera.mitre.org/) is a platform for adversary emulation inside a charge that has already been given. It is an instrument for running a resemblance. It is not a liturgy, and this essay will not teach it. Cody Thomas's [Funnel of Fidelity](https://specterops.io/blog/2019/11/20/introducing-the-funnel-of-fidelity/) asks how closely an emulation ought to resemble the adversary it claims to resemble, from the aim down toward the particular act. I cite it as a question of honesty about the instrument. A test that claims a closeness it did not attempt is a kind of theater. A test that attempts a closeness the charge did not grant is a kind of theft. The funnel is not a doctrine of providence. It is a way of not lying about what the resemblance was.

[Geneva](https://github.com/AthanasiusXOR/geneva-ludus) is where I will let that resemblance be practiced in public, on a range and not in a neighbor's house. Even there, success is not a sign that God has taken sides. A range that "works" has returned what it was built to return. Providence is not a green badge.

### Theater

Bruce Schneier has a public name for a cousin of this vice. In [Beyond Security Theater](https://www.schneier.com/essays/archives/2009/11/beyond_security_thea.html) he describes measures that perform the feeling of security without the substance. The cousin is real, and keepers should hate it: the control that exists to be seen, the ritual that photographs well and stops nothing. I mean an additional theater, and I do not want the two confused. The theological theater is the finding staged as a judgment. The house is not merely shown a door. It is sentenced. The tester assigns humiliation, ranks the persons, chooses a tone that will make a board afraid, and calls the fear providence. If the truth humbles a house, that is between the house and God. Our part is to tell the truth without contempt.

Isaiah's account of Assyria is the warning. God uses a wicked instrument and then judges the instrument for the pride of thinking its own hand did it. A tester is not Assyria. The pattern still cuts. You can be useful under a providence you do not control, and you can ruin yourself by reading usefulness as appointment. "God meant it for good" is not a line you get to say over a person you have decided to crush. Joseph said it as the brother who had suffered, not as the attacker who wanted a text. If you reach for it to decorate a severe report, you are recruiting a verse the way the project recruits a mascot. Put it down. Say what was returned. Spare the sermon that makes you the narrator of God's intention.

### What providence frees you from

It frees you from the panic that you are the last governor of the house. You are not. A missed finding is not a hole in the decree. A severe finding is not your sentence, carried out. The confession's comfort is that God governs the engagement and the thief the engagement resembles, and the liberty of second causes besides. You still answer for your own will. Providence is not permission. It is the reason you can tell the truth and sleep, without the theater, because the outcome was never yours to secure by fright.

Do the work you were given. Resemble only what you were authorized to resemble. Say how close the resemblance actually came. Return the account. Refuse the punishment you were not given to assign. God was already governing the room. He did not need your badge, and he will not wear it.
