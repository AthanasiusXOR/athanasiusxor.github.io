> "They show that the work of the law is written on their hearts, while their conscience also bears witness."
> ~ **Romans 2:15**

There is a story this craft tells when it wants to begin at the policy. The method, it says, is a tool. Tools are morally empty. The emptiness lasts until a lawyer, a contract, or a framework arrives and pours a rule in. Before that hour the operator is a pair of hands. After that hour he is compliant. The story is convenient. It is also a doctrine of the law, and it is the wrong one.

Paul says the work of the law is written on the heart. He is talking about Gentiles who do not have the Mosaic tablets and who nevertheless do, by nature, things the law requires. Their conscience bears witness. Thoughts accuse or excuse. This is not saving knowledge. The Second London Confession is plain that the light of nature does not give the knowledge of God and of his will that is necessary for salvation, and that the moral law, delivered on Sinai, and summarized by Christ, binds all persons in all ages. The point for this work is narrower and sharp enough. Conscience is already speaking before the statement of work is signed. A method is an act of a person. Persons are not empty until counsel arrives.

### Downstream, and still necessary

I am not despising the documents. I am putting them in the order the confession already has.

The [(ISC)² Code of Ethics](https://www.isc2.org/ethics) is a professional rule: protect society, act honorably, provide diligent service, advance the profession. It can be signed by a man who does not fear God, and it can be kept, in its own terms, by that man. It is a real bond inside a guild. It is downstream of an older knowledge. The guild did not invent the duty not to prey. It inherited a hint of it and wrote the hint in the dialect of a credential.

The [National Cyber Security Centre's account of penetration testing](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/penetration-testing) tells an organization, in public, what an authorized test is and what a buyer should be asking before anyone is let in. [NIST's guide to information security testing and assessment](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final) is the American document many houses use when they try to say, in advance, how a test may be conducted and how it may not. These are circumstances of a common life, the sort the confession says are to be ordered by the light of nature and Christian prudence, under the general rules of the Word. They are not the Word. A man can satisfy the guide and still have used a person as material. The guide did not fail. It was never the law.

The magistrate has spoken as well. In the United States the statute most often named in this room is [18 U.S.C. § 1030](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1030). I am not giving legal advice, and I am not offering a way through the statute. I am naming the order the Epistle already named. God is first. The civil magistrate is next, in the sphere God gave him. A client's enthusiasm does not repeal either. Where the law of the land forbids the act, a signature does not baptize it. The tester who treats the statute as the first moral fact he has ever met has simply not been listening to his own conscience. The tester who treats the statute as a puzzle to be stood next to, rather than as a governor, has mistaken citizenship for tradecraft.

### What conscience already knows

It knows that persons and their goods are not raw material. It knows this before it knows the word "scope." A name, a balance, a diagnosis, a prayer, a child's photograph: these accuse the man who handles them as puzzle pieces, and they accuse him in the moment, not at the outbrief. The accusation can be silenced. Paul says men suppress the truth. A busy engagement is a very good silencer. So is a room that laughs. So is a doctrine that calls the method empty until the paperwork catches up. The paperwork then arrives and ratifies a conscience that has already agreed to be late.

It knows that skill increases duty. The ability to enter quietly is not a moral credit. It is a weight. The man who can take a thing without being seen is more accountable for the taking, not less. Professional rules matter because they say this in public where a guild can discipline a member. They are right to say it. They are right because the older law was already right. An outpost that starts where the policy starts will never ask why the policy had to be written. It will argue about clauses. It will not ask about the neighbor.

It knows the difference between a ruse aimed at a control the keeper asked to have tried, and a lie told to the keeper himself. I will not pretend the edge of speech is easy. Augustine's severity about falsehood and the later arguments about public ruse are a real argument, and this page will not counterfeit a settlement. The line that is not inside that argument is the line conscience already draws. The truth is owed to the person who asked for the truth. A fiction that survives into the report, or that is aimed at a neighbor who did not ask to be the exercise, is not a clever circumstance. It is false witness. The code of ethics can say "act honorably" and still leave a coward room to hide. The law on the heart does not leave that room. It accuses.

### The use of the documents

Use them. Write the mandate. Read the guide. Know what the magistrate has forbidden. Sign nothing that asks you to do evil that good may come. Then do not make the signature the origin of your morals. The origin is older than the firm, older than the framework, older than you. Christ summarized the law as love of God and love of neighbor. Paul says love is the fulfilling of the law, and that love does no wrong to a neighbor. A clean appendix that wrongs a neighbor is a clean appendix. It is not obedience.

Christian prudence still has work to do, because the documents really are circumstances. How long the test lasts, which systems are in bounds, what must be stopped at once, what a congregation can bear: these are not printed in Romans. They are ordered under Romans. The operator who despises NIST because he has read Paul is not spiritual. He is undisciplined. The operator who despises Paul because he has read NIST is not professional. He is late to a conversation his conscience started without him.

The law of God returns to this craft as that order. The method was never empty. The lawyer was never the first speaker. The code is a gift when it keeps a guild from lying about the duty. It becomes an alibi when it is asked to be the duty's source. You already knew the neighbor was not material. The contract should have found you knowing it.
