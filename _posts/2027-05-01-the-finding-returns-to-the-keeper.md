Sovereignty is sometimes used as a pillow. If the Lord governs all things, the hole will be found when he wishes, and the report can wait. Or, darker, the tester's hands were the hands of providence, so the entry needs no grant. Both sentences make God the author of a neglect or a theft. He is not. The plate at the top puts the finding on the path to the wall. The crack beside it is the way the finding gets kept in the dark.

> "But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the trumpet, so that the people are not warned, and the sword comes and takes any one of them, that person is taken away in his iniquity, but his blood I will require at the watchman's hand."
> ~ **Ezekiel 33:6**

## The realm has a keeper

The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness of it. A company, a church, a household of systems is a realm only in the small sense. It is a charge, with edges, held by stewards who must give an account. Their realm is that charge. It is not a private cosmos, and the red team does not become lord of it by learning where it is weak.

The defenders are the guard set on that wall. Call them blue, or the security team, or the elders and the people they have charged with the systems. The name can change with the house. The office does not. They are the ones who have to recognize the thing, close it, and watch the next night. A finding that never reaches them has not yet done the work the engagement was for.

The confession says God, in his ordinary providence, makes use of means. He is free to work beyond them. Neglect of the lawful means is not faith. The watch on a wall is one of those means. So is the report that tells the watch where to stand.

## The trumpet is the report

The watchman who sees the sword and stays silent has not been humble about his knowledge. He has hidden it. In this trade the trumpet is the report to the keeper and to the people charged with the defense. It names the weakness at the altitude of judgment. What failed. Under what conditions it matters. What the repair has to accomplish. It does not become a story for the public. It does not stay in the tester's pocket as proof of skill.

Red informs blue because the emulation was for their discernment. The form of the telling can fit the house: a readout, a joint review, a list the defender can act on. The duty is the same in every form. If the defenders learn of the hole only when a stranger uses it, the engagement was a private demonstration. The test that knocks the realm over and then explains has made the warning more expensive than the sword. Operational care is part of the trumpet. A warning that arrives as rubble is a late warning.

During the engagement the two crafts owe each other the truth their roles allow. The defenders should not be left to suffer an unknown disaster when the activity is the test they authorized. The testers should not wander outside the grant and call the wandering thoroughness. People call the practical habit deconfliction. The moral name is older. The realm has one keeper, and both crafts are servants of that keeper's defense.

## Providence uses the guard

Nehemiah prayed and set a guard. The prayer did not retire the watch, and the watch did not retire the prayer. A vulnerability returned to the defender is a means in the Lord's government of that house. A vulnerability kept back is a falsehood the architecture goes on telling. This field-post has already said that the engagement exists so the falsehood can be retired. The retirement happens in the realm, by the people whose realm it is.

When the realm is a congregation, the keepers are the elders, and the weakness touches neighbors. Mail, giving, counsel, and names are not a second church. They are the charge. The trumpet is still owed, and it is owed in confidence. A breach story is not content. The blood on the watchman's hands, in Ezekiel's sentence, is what silence costs. The sentence should be heavy enough to end the habit of saving the best detail for the talk afterward.

How a weakness is turned into an entry, and how a particular defense is built, do not belong on this field-post. They belong inside the authorized charge, with a scope and a way to undo the damage. The judgment belongs here. See the sword. Sound the trumpet where the guard can hear it. Leave the keeper in possession of the realm. The Lord remains Lord of the house you were allowed to examine.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
