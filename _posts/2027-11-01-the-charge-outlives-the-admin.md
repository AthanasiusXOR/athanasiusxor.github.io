Someone leaves. The person who knew the weakness, or the person who held the access in his head, is no longer in the room. The house is still there. The plate at the top sends the seal along the path to the keepers. The charge is arriving where it belongs. It is not in a pocket on the way out.

> "This is how one should regard us, as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God. Moreover, it is required of stewards that they be found faithful."
> ~ **1 Corinthians 4:1–2**

## Not an inheritance

A Reformed church does not hand a congregation down as private property. The charge outlives the officer. Elders die, move, and finish a term. The flock remains, and the flock is received by shepherds who can give an account, not by a dynasty that kept the secrets in one head. The security of a house follows the same order, at a respectful distance. The realm is a charge. The person who administered it was a steward. When the steward leaves, faithfulness is whether the next steward can take up the work without inheriting a riddle.

The offensive engagement makes this sharp. The operator learned where the house fails. He may have been granted a way in for the length of the test. That knowledge and that way are not wages. They are not a story he owns, and they are not a key he keeps because he was clever enough to be trusted with it. Faithfulness returns the knowledge in the report and ends the access when the grant ends. What travels with the operator, unspoken and still valid, is an inheritance he was never given.

## A dynasty of one head

The same failure wears an employee's badge. The administrator who will not write the charge down, who keeps the paths in memory, who treats a vacation as a crisis because nobody else can be faithful with the house, has made himself the heir. The church version is the volunteer who holds the mail, the giving, and the names, and who answers a question about them with "only I know." Plurality of elders exists so the flock is not one person's secret. A single head with all the access is a bishop the congregation did not call.

Both dynasties feel like care. The operator says he is protecting the finding by keeping it close. The admin says he is protecting the house by being indispensable. The sheep experience it as a charge that cannot be received by anyone else. When the dynasty is sick, or proud, or simply finished, the house discovers it has no steward. It has a vacancy and a locked door.

## Found faithful

What the keepers should be able to see is ordinary, and it is the whole of the public claim. Who holds access now. What the last engagement opened, and whether that opening is dead. Where the knowledge of the weakness was written, and whether the people who remain can read it. A charge that lives only in a departed person is a charge that was never handed on.

How access is taken, copied, or kept alive does not belong on this field-post. The judgment does. Stewards are required to be found faithful. Faithful here is a charge the next hands can hold. The seal in the plate is on the path to the keepers. A seal in the pocket of the person leaving is the dynasty, already packed.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
