A single moment is a thin thing to hang a program on. One alert. One note in a tester's book. One picture of a door that was open for a minute. Israel did not establish a charge that way. The plate at the top is the rule in the room. One seal sits by itself. The charge stands where the witnesses stand together.

> "A single witness shall not suffice against a person for any crime or for any wrong in connection with any offense that he has committed. Only on the evidence of two witnesses or of three witnesses shall a charge be established."
> ~ **Deuteronomy 19:15**

## The second witness

The offensive finding is a claim that something in the house failed under conditions the keeper allowed the team to press. A claim of that weight is not a mood and not a trophy. It is established when it can be shown again. The tester's account and the defender's record. The condition and the log of the condition. The path and the fact that the path answers a second time inside the grant, without surprising the people who must live with the proof.

This is not a demand for theater. Two witnesses are enough. Three are enough. The point of the law was to refuse a lone voice the power to ruin a neighbor, and to refuse the community the luxury of ignoring a charge that had been corroborated. Both refusals belong in a readout. A rumor does not get to discipline a program, spend a budget, or name a person as careless. A corroborated finding does not get to be filed as folklore.

What the witnesses look like in a given house will vary. The judgment does not. If the finding cannot be shown to the keeper without asking the keeper to trust the tester's word alone, it is not yet a charge. Say so. Go and establish it, or withdraw it. September's older essay already said a finding is demonstrated or it is a rumor. Demonstration is more than one voice agreeing with itself.

## The witness who never arrives

The other abuse of the law is delay. A keeper, or a vendor, or a tired room asks for one more export, one more reproduction, one more meeting, until the trumpet is postponed into the next quarter. Deuteronomy did not say to wait for a crowd. It said a charge is established at two or three. Endless witnesses are how a true finding is buried politely. The people asking for them are often asking to be excused from acting.

There is a pastoral shape to this, and it should stay in its own place. The church does not receive an accusation against an elder on one voice, and the church does not hide behind procedure when the witnesses have spoken. A security program is not a church court. It is still a place where a person's name and a neighbor's safety can be touched by a careless charge or by a buried one. The same fear of a lone voice, and the same refusal of endless delay, keep the work from becoming either slander or slumber.

How a condition is shown does not belong on this field-post. The judgment does. One seal is not a charge. Two are. After two, the hearing is the point. The next essay's hireling is the one who brings a charge and will not stay to be questioned. The witness who establishes a finding owes the keeper his presence until the finding is understood.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
