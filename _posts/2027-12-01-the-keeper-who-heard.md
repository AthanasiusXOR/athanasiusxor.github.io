The trumpet was blown. The readout happened. The owner and the defenders heard the path, the conditions, and the repair. Then the meeting ended and the house stayed as it was. The plate at the top leaves the horn on the floor. The gate remains open. Hearing, in this picture, has already occurred.

> "He heard the sound of the trumpet and did not take warning; his blood shall be upon himself. But if he had taken warning, he would have saved his life."
> ~ **Ezekiel 33:5**

## The other half

May belonged to the watchman. The red team that sees the weakness and keeps it, or sounds it where the guard cannot hear, has the blood of silence on its hands. This essay will not repeat that charge. Ezekiel has a second person in the same passage. The people hear the horn and do not take warning. Their blood is on themselves. The offensive assessment can be faithful and still be filed. A perfect trumpet does not move a keeper who has decided to stay indoors.

The ways of staying indoors are respectable. The finding is acknowledged. A ticket is opened. A quarter is named. The risk is accepted in a sentence that was not acceptance so much as adjournment. February's distinction belongs here again. The repair was promised in the meeting. It was not made in the house. The keeper heard a promise and treated the hearing as the work.

## What hearing is

Hearing, in Ezekiel's sentence, is taking warning. The life is saved by the warning that is acted on, not by the warning that is praised. In a realm under stewardship the action has a shape the keeper can be asked about later. The door the assessment named is shut, or the keeper has said in truth why it remains open and what watch stands in front of it. The access the test used is dead. The control that was a slide is now a thing in the tenant. The defenders know where to stand tonight, not where the report suggested they might stand next year.

None of that requires panic, and none of it requires the testers to seize the house. The keeper remains the keeper. Taking warning is his act. A red team that tries to become the repair, past the grant, has become a second administration. A keeper that praises the red team and does not move has become the hearer in the text. The two failures are different. Both leave the gate open.

## The next stranger

The person who comes later, without a grant, does not need the report. He needs the path the report named. If the path is still there, the assessment was a dress rehearsal he did not have to attend. The blood on the hearer is a hard sentence, and it should stay hard. It is not a license for the testers to despise the client. It is the end of the habit of filing a true warning as though filing were faith.

How the later stranger walks the path does not belong on this field-post. The judgment does. The horn has been blown. It is on the floor in the plate because its work, as sound, is finished. The gate is the remaining question. A keeper who heard and took warning can point to a house that changed. A keeper who heard and adjourned can point to a document. Ezekiel already said which of those saves a life.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
