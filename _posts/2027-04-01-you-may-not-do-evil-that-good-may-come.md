People defend a rough test by the peace they hope will follow. The house will be safer. The defenders will learn. The sentence that has to be answered first is older than the trade. The plate at the top is that sentence in one room. The straight way reaches the good. The other path breaks before the gate.

> "And why not do evil that good may come?—as some people slanderously charge us with saying. Their condemnation is just."
> ~ **Romans 3:8**

## The law written on the heart

When Gentiles who do not have Sinai do what the law requires, Paul says they show the work of the law written on their hearts. Conscience accuses, and conscience excuses. That is natural law in Scripture's own words. It is God's moral requirement, known in the creature. It is not a way of salvation, and it is not a second covenant beside Christ.

The older teachers called this the rational creature's share in God's eternal law. The confession keeps a plainer order, and the confession is the standard. God gave Adam a law of obedience, written in the heart. After the fall that same moral law was delivered on Sinai. It binds every person. Christ in the gospel does not dissolve the obligation. He strengthens it. The new covenant writes the law on the heart of a forgiven people. It does not retire the duty, and it does not offer the duty as a righteousness.

For this work the duty is plain. Another's house, the machines that carry it, and the names of the people who depend on them are not raw material. To enter without a real grant is theft, however educational the entry feels. To take a name, a confidence, or a record and keep it is theft a second time. To break what you were trusted to examine is a failed stewardship, even when the narrative is exciting. The five guardrails already published on this field-post are that duty written as conditions. A trade that calls itself an exception is a conscience in the act of excusing.

## What the newer account sees

In our own decades a school called the new natural law, associated with Germain Grisez and John Finnis, has restated the tradition around basic goods. Life, knowledge, friendship, and the others stand as reasons for action that are not merely private tastes. The rule this essay will not surrender is theirs in one form and Paul's in an older form. You may not intend a harm to a real good as the means to your end.

Knowledge is a real good. So is the peace of the people in the house. So is truthfulness about what the test found. A penetration test is finished when the substance of a control is known. A red team engagement is a bounded emulation, so the defenders can see their own discernment under pressure. In both, the act intends that knowledge and the repair it serves. It does not intend the injury, the spectacle, or the stolen name. Harm that can be avoided is refused. Harm that cannot be avoided, if the truth is to be known at all, is kept small, told at once, and undone.

The newer account is thinner than the confession where it lets those goods float free of the God whose law they are, and where religion can be ranked as one good beside the others rather than the creature's duty to the Lord. Take the thinness as a warning. Take the rule. The good end is the reason the means must stay clean. It is not a baptism for a dirty means.

The person on the other end of the test is a neighbor. A finding that needs someone's humiliation as the product has chosen against the good it claims to serve. A report that softens the truth so the room stays comfortable has chosen against knowledge. Flattery and cruelty are both lies about the house.

## The grant is the boundary

Authorization is the keeper of the realm saying, in words that can be shown, what may be touched, how far, and for how long. Curiosity is not a covenant. A wave across a conference table is not a grant. A grant to look at one house is not a grant to follow the thread into a neighbor's house, a vendor's house, or the private life of a person who only works there.

A granted test is the steward examining his own charge, by a servant he has sent. The wrong is the entry, the taking, or the destruction outside that charge, and the use of the charge for a reason the steward did not give. Skill does not create the grant. A predicted safety does not repair a means the law already forbids.

The public claim stops where this field-post always stops. How an intrusion is carried out does not belong here. The judgment does. If the means are wrong, the defense you hope for will not make them right. If the means are lawful, the work is still a stewardship. Tell the truth. Spare what you can. Put the finding back in the hands of the people who have to live in the house. The law that accuses a trespass accuses the tester too. Keeping a scope is not a righteousness. It is obedience in a small room, and it is owed.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
