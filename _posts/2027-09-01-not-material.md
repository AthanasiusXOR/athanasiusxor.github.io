> "So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them."
> ~ **Genesis 1:27**
>
> "Whoever sheds the blood of man, by man shall his blood be shed, for God made man in his own image."
> ~ **Genesis 9:6**

The image is the reason the blood is not cheap. Genesis 9 does not locate the worth of a man in his usefulness, his innocence, or his ability to keep a secret. It locates the worth in the image. James says the tongue curses people who are made in the likeness of God, and he treats that as a contradiction of blessing God (James 3:9). The fall mars the image. It does not repeal it. The Second London Confession says God endowed man with knowledge, righteousness, and true holiness, after his own image, and that the fall left us dead in sin and wholly defiled. Defiled is not erased. The neighbor in the data is still the neighbor. He is not material.

A record is not a person. That sentence protects the person from being confused with a file, and it protects the file from being worshipped as if it were the soul. Both confusions are available to this craft. One treats the export as the man, and then handles the man with the manners proper to an export. The other treats the record as sacred in itself, a relic, and forgets that the relic matters because of the living person it can expose, injure, or strip. The image sits on the person. The record is a creaturely trace. What you can reach is not what you may use. What you may use, inside the charge, you may not carry out as a story.

### Reach is not license

The test will put traces in your hands. Names, balances, notes, photographs, the shape of a household, the fact that someone asked a pastor for help. Some of that will arrive because you were told to see whether it could arrive. The arrival is not a change in the person's status. He was the image of God before you could read the row, and he is the image of God after. Capability has never been the measure of lawful use. The serpent's question was a question about a permitted reach. The craft's question is the same question in a worse font. Seeing that you can is not the same sentence as seeing that you may.

The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) is a public way for an institution to ask what it collects, why, who may have it, and how little will do. It is not a doctrine of the image. It is a circumstance under the doctrine. A Christian can be glad of the circumstance and still tell the truth about its ceiling. A framework can require minimization. It cannot tell you why minimization is owed. Minimization is owed because the person is not raw material for a complete picture, not even a picture gathered under a contract. The charge may require you to prove that a trace can be reached. It rarely requires you to dwell, to copy the dwelling into a trophy, or to repeat it until the room laughs. Prove the reach. Stop. The extra looking is not thoroughness. It is use. Use of a person, past the charge, is what the image forbids.

[(ISC)²'s code](https://www.isc2.org/ethics) says, in the guild's words, to protect society and the common good and to act honorably. Good. The honor has a face. The face is not yours. When a code and a doctrine seem to conflict, do not be romantic about breaking the code, and do not be professional about breaking the doctrine. In this case they do not conflict. The code is the thinner sentence. The image is the reason the thinner sentence was right.

### The story is a second use

The first misuse is in the engagement: copying what you do not need, widening what you were shown, keeping a set after the access was supposed to die. The second misuse is afterward, and it is the one the guild applauds. The story. A detail from a person's life, told because it makes a talk land, or a blog feel real, or a dinner table go quiet. The detail was never yours. Confidentiality is the form love takes when the finding is ugly. It binds a public account as strictly as it binds a report. Changing the name is not enough if the household is still recognizable, and it is not enough, morally, if the household is not recognizable but you are still spending a person's pain as seasoning.

There is a place where practice does not need the flock's names at all. [Geneva](https://github.com/AthanasiusXOR/geneva-ludus) is a range, populated by the fiction a lab requires, so that a method can be seen without a neighbor sitting inside the method. That is the right hunger for realism: realism about the craft, not consumption of the real. When a charge does put a real name in your hands, the name is a trust. You return the fact of the exposure to the keeper. You do not return the person to the audience.

### The image, after the fall

I do not ground this in the person's goodness. The image is why the blood of a sinner is still not cheap. Genesis 9 is after the fall and after the flood. The men who bear the image are the same race that filled the earth with violence. You will meet records of people who have done harm, and records of people who have been harmed, and you will not always know which is which from the row. The doctrine does not ask you to declare them innocent. It asks you not to treat them as stock. Judgment of a person, where it is called for, belongs to God and to the magistrates he has given, not to the man who happened to open the table. Your office is smaller. Tell the keeper what the boundary allowed. Leave the person in his hands, and in God's.

The mirror in the work is a good test. If the glass shows you a case, a ticket, a proof, you have already lost the person. If it shows you a neighbor, you may still do the charged thing, and you will do less of it, and you will not tell it later as if it had been a sport. God made man in his own image. He did not make him a fixture of your report.
