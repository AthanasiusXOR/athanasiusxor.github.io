The engagement ends. Someone asks if they passed. The paper can be made clean enough to frame. The house is a different question. The plate at the top holds them apart. The gate is the house. The frame is the paper.

> "And to the one who does not work but believes in him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is counted as righteousness."
> ~ **Romans 4:5**

## Counted

Justification is God's act. He counts a sinner righteous in Christ, not because the sinner assembled a record that would survive inspection. The confession keeps that sentence where it belongs, on the sinner and the Son. An assessment does not sit in that courtroom. A passed report imputes nothing. It does not make the house righteous, and it does not make the team righteous for having written it.

The older error on this field-post was likeness counted as the thing itself. A signed host counted as the library. A diagram counted as the tenant. A control that only resembles the thing it claims. The audit is that error in a binder. The grade is clean. The gate is open. The paper has been asked to do the work of righteousness, and paper cannot do it.

## What the work showed

The offensive work, inside a grant, is a demonstration. A path was walked, or a path was stopped. Those are facts about the house under the conditions of the test. They are not a verdict on the persons, and they are not a sacrament that makes the next year safe. Getting in is news the keeper needed. Being stopped is news the keeper needed. The sentence that turns either fact into "satisfactory" has changed the genre. The demonstration became a grade. The grade became a covering.

The place this happens is familiar. The executive letter is calm. The findings sit in an appendix, lowered by a word like theoretical after the path was walked, or accepted by a person who wanted the meeting to end. The control that fired in the lab and slept in the realm is described as present. September's essay already said a finding is demonstrated or it is a rumor. This one adds the use that is made of a demonstration. It can be told. It can also be framed until the frame is what the keeper remembers.

## The distance

The tester is not justified by a clean scope, a clever path, or a harsh report. The client is not condemned by a finding as though the finding were the last day. The report's work is smaller and it is owed. Tell the truth about the house. Leave the righteousness where Romans put it.

What the keeper should be able to see is the demonstration beside the grade, the way the plate sets the gate beside the frame. If the paper cannot be laid on the house without embarrassment, the paper is the finding. How a path is walked does not belong on this field-post. The judgment does. A resemblance that has been counted as righteousness will be trusted by people the test already informed. The house will still be the house.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
