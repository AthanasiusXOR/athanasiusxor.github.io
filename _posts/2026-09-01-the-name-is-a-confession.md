A name can be a joke, a brand, or a vow. This one is a vow. If the epistle is the charter, this is the signature under it.

> "Am I now seeking the approval of man, or of God? Or am I trying to please man? If I were still trying to please man, I would not be a servant of Christ."
> ~ **Galatians 1:10**

## The bishop

Athanasius of Alexandria spent the fourth century refusing a smaller Christ. The fashionable account said the Son was *like* God, exalted, ancient, and still a creature. Athanasius said no. Likeness is not identity. A savior who is almost God is not a savior. He was exiled for it, more than once, and the line that stuck to him was *contra mundum* — against the world. Not against people. Against the demand that truth shrink so the room stays comfortable.

I did not take the name because I think I am a church father. I took it because the temptation he refused is ordinary in my trade. The dashboard looks like defense. The product sheet sounds like assurance. The control is present in the diagram and absent in the kernel. Almost-secure is the Arian offer, updated and invoiced.

## The gate

XOR is the other half, and it is not a flourish. Exclusive or returns true when the inputs disagree. It is a small, hard gate. In theology and in code I keep meeting the same pressure: add a third state, call the contradiction a nuance, and ship it. There are nuances. There is not a middle value between a true God and a made god, or between a control that holds and a control that only resembles one.

The headphones on the portrait are not a bit. They are the job. I live in packet captures and in creeds, and I do not intend to become fluent in one by going quiet in the other. The Southern Seminary degree sits next to the tooling on purpose. A mind that can parse a creed and cannot read a log will bless what it does not understand. A mind that can read a log and will not be questioned by a creed will call every technique neutral.

## What the name rules out

Three refusals follow from the signature.

1. **No costume orthodoxy.** Quoting Nicaea over a sloppy engagement does not make the engagement true. The creed judges the work. It is not a texture for the slide.
2. **No middle report.** A finding is demonstrated or it is a rumor. A system is set apart from the thing it claims to stop, or it is not. "Similar" is how heresies and breaches both introduce themselves.
3. **No audience-shaped gospel.** Galatians 1:10 is not a license to be rude. It is a warning against editing the truth so the room will hire you. Clients get a clear report. They do not get a smaller Christ, and they do not get a softer finding, because either edit would make me someone else.

## Contra mundum, in practice

Against the world, for a red team, is a narrow phrase and easy to spoil. It does not mean contempt for defenders, scorn for blue teams, or a romance with the breach. The world, in the old sense, is the arrangement that rewards appearance over substance. A report that tells the truth and then stays to help the repair is not contra mundum. A report that flatters the appearance is.

I am AthanasiusXOR. The name is the standard I fail and return to. Creed and code. No third state.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
