The assessment finds the crack. That is what an offensive test is for. Clay breaks. The surprise is not the crack. The surprise is what people preach about it. The plate at the top is a jar on the steel, light coming through the break. The light is the point of the crack. The jar is still a jar.

> "But we have this treasure in jars of clay, to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us."
> ~ **2 Corinthians 4:7**

## The treasure is not the jar

Paul's treasure is the gospel. The jar is the apostle, frail on purpose, so the power is seen to belong to God. A church's systems are a much smaller jar. They carry preaching, giving, counsel, and names. They are not the gospel, and a finding about them is not a finding about Christ. The team that treats a breach as the failure of the faith has confused the vessel with the treasure. The keeper that treats a clean dashboard as the power of God has made the same confusion from the other side.

The offensive work tells the truth about the clay. The stack can be entered. The backup can be empty. The names can be copied. None of those sentences unpreaches Christ, and none of them is a small thing, because the jar was holding a neighbor's trust. The distinction keeps the team from contempt and the church from despair. The gospel does not ride on the uptime. The neighbors still do.

## Fragility is not a shrug

"Everything is clay" can be said in Paul's voice or in a lazy one. Paul's voice spends the frailty so that God's power is not mistaken for the vessel. The lazy voice spends the frailty so that nobody has to tend the vessel. An unguarded jar is a stewardship failure with a congregation attached. The August essay on this field-post already said that. The crack the test reveals is a call to tend, not a doctrine that tending is unspiritual.

There is a vanity that goes the other way. A church can spend itself trying to make the jar glorious, a stack so impressive that the treasure is no longer what anyone notices. The surpassing power belongs to God. A security program that exists to make the church feel omnipotent has forgotten the verse while buying tools in its name. The jar should be sound enough to carry what it carries. It should not be preached as the power.

## What the test is allowed to say

The public claim stops at recognition. The offensive finding may say: here is the crack, here is what could be lost through it, here is the repair that would let the jar keep carrying. It may not say that the congregation failed God by being made of people, and it may not say that the crack is none of anyone's business because the treasure is spiritual. How the crack is opened does not belong on this page. The judgment does. Light through the break is a mercy when it shows the keeper where to lay a hand. It is a cruelty when the team holds the jar up only to prove that clay was always going to fail.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
