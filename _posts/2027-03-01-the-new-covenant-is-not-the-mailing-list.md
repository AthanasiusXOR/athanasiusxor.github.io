The stack a church keeps is real work. Someone has to carry the mail, record the gifts, file the counsel, and know the names of the people who cannot afford to be known by strangers. That work is stewardship. The plate at the top keeps it in its place. A table, a cup, bread, the thing Christ called the covenant in his blood. Beside it, a safe of letters. The safe can serve the table. The plate's line is the short form of the claim. Not the list.

> "Pay careful attention to yourselves and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to care for the church of God, which he obtained with his own blood."
> ~ **Acts 20:28**

## The list is a servant

Jeremiah's promise was that they shall all know the Lord, from the least of them to the greatest, because he will forgive their iniquity. The new covenant community is the community of the forgiven. Baptism marks a profession of that forgiveness. The table is for those who are in Christ. Membership, when it is honest, names those people so the church can care for them and, when it must, correct them. A platform does not do that naming. The church does.

![A ring of lamps on the stone, the people who know the Lord.](/assets/img/site/the-flock.jpg)

A mailing list can reach the members. A giving record can tell the truth about money. A counseling file can keep a confidence so a person is not retold in the hallway. A directory can help a deacon find the household that needs bread. These are good servants. They become a counterfeit when the church speaks of them as if they were the people. A list can hold the forgiven, the curious, and the long-gone, and it will not say which is which. Jeremiah will. The font will. The list will say who can be reached.

This field-post has refused a second people running beside Christ. It should refuse a second church running inside the software. The visible church is word, baptism, the table, and the discipline of a congregation that knows its own. The stack is how a few stewards carry mail and money and names. The new covenant is actually new. It is made of persons who know the Lord. Rows that can be exported are a tool those persons may use.

## What the elders should be able to see

The public claim stops at recognition. The burden named on this field-post still stands. The systems that carry preaching, giving, counsel, and the names of the vulnerable are a stewardship with a congregation attached.

The elders should be able to answer four questions without asking a vendor to explain their own house.

Who can read the names, and who can copy them out. Who can send mail as the church. Where the gifts are recorded, and who can move that record. Whether counsel sits in a drawer the whole staff can open, or with the few who were trusted with it.

A wide answer is already a finding. The directory of a congregation is a care for neighbors. The names of the harmed, the poor, and the children are neighbors in that directory. Giving is the record of a gift. Counsel is a trust. Oversight is the elder's work, and sight is how oversight stays honest. A tool the elders cannot inspect is a flock they have handed to someone they cannot question.

How those systems are broken open, how a letter is made to sound like the church, and how a directory is carried off do not belong on this field-post. They belong, when they belong at all, inside an authorized engagement with a scope and a way to undo the damage. The judgment belongs here. A church that cannot say who holds the names is already late to its own duty.

## A smaller circle

The repair is a smaller circle of access. Named people. A way to know when the list is copied, and when mail goes out as the church from someone the elders did not charge. Confidentiality was already one of the five guardrails. In a congregation it has faces.

Peter tells the elders to shepherd willingly, and not for shameful gain. The gain can be as small as a dashboard. The list as reach. The wounded as a file anyone on staff can browse. The gifts as a number that flatters the front page. Willingness is the opposite posture. The steward stays because the people are neighbors.

The cup is the new covenant in his blood. The safe is paper, steel, and an account on someone else's computer. Serve the table with the safe. The people know the Lord, or they do not. An export will not settle it.

**Contra mundum. Code obscurum. Christ supreme.**
